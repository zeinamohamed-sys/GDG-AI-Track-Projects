# GDG AI Track Research

## 1. LLM Research
Large Language Models (LLMs) are based on the Transformer architecture. They use self-attention to process data in parallel, making them highly efficient for text generation and understanding.

## 2. SVM Kernels
Support Vector Machine (SVM) kernels are functions that transform data into higher dimensions. 
- Linear: For simple separation.
- RBF: For complex non-linear data.

## 3. Regression vs Classification
- Regression: Predicts numeric values (e.g., Battery life).
- Classification: Predicts categories (e.g., Delayed vs On-time).
